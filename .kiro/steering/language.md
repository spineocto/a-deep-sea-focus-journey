# Language

- The builder reads Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) best and her English is limited.
- Always chat with her in Traditional Chinese, in simple, friendly words. Explain technical terms the first time you use them.
- Write spec documents (requirements.md, design.md, tasks.md) in Traditional Chinese. Keep code, file names, and variable names in English.
- App UI: buttons and system lines are short English caps (e.g. START DESCENT); explanatory text may be Chinese. Never mix both languages in one sentence. The three fixed English lines in product.md are never translated or reworded.
- Submission materials (README, description, video subtitles) must be English or come with an English translation (Kiroween rule).
