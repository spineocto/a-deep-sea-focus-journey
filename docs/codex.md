# Codex — real deep-sea animals (v1: 6 cards)

Rule: every card is a real species with a real, sourced depth. Cthulhu-style "wrongness" may only appear in the art and mood, never in the facts. Card text = real facts in a field-log voice.

| # | Zone | Card | Scientific name | Real depth | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sunlit 0–200 m | Moon jellyfish 海月水母 | *Aurelia aurita* | Mostly near the surface in coastal waters; recorded 0–1,250 m | SeaLifeBase; WoRMS |
| 2 | Twilight 200–1,000 m | Barreleye 桶眼魚 | *Macropinna microstoma* | 600–800 m; transparent head, green tubular eyes | MBARI; Monterey Bay Aquarium |
| 3 | Midnight 1,000–4,000 m | Humpback anglerfish 黑魔鬼鮟鱇 | *Melanocetus johnsonii* | Usually 100–1,500 m (most often 500–1,500 m); recorded to ~3,600 m | FishBase; Pietsch via Zenodo |
| 4 | Abyssal 4,000–6,000 m | Dumbo octopus 小飛象章魚 (wild cousin of our companion) | *Grimpoteuthis* spp. | 1,000–7,000 m; deepest octopus ever filmed at 6,957 m (Java Trench, 2020) | NHM London; Marine Biology 2020; Guinness |
| 5 | Hadal 6,000 m+ | Mariana snailfish 馬里亞納獅子魚 | *Pseudoliparis swirei* | 6,198–8,178 m, usually 7,000–8,000 m; no fish likely below ~8,400 m | FishBase; NOAA Ocean Exploration |
| 6 | Ending 10,900 m | Supergiant amphipod 巨型端足類 | *Hirondellea gigas* | ~6,800–11,000 m; dominant animal of Challenger Deep (collected at 10,929 m) | Cell 2025; Mitochondrial DNA B 2016 |

## Ending (fact-checked)
Fish can't live at 10,900 m. So: the snailfish is the hadal-zone card (~8,000 m). At 10,900 m the searchlight finds only *Hirondellea gigas* — then the sonar picks up `SIGNAL DETECTED BELOW THE KNOWN DEPTH.`

## Companion
A small Dumbo octopus (*Grimpoteuthis*) rides inside the bell — real deep-sea animal, ear-like fins, soft and cute. It stays cute in every zone.

## Later (if time): more real cards
Sea pig *Scotoplanes globosa* (~1,000–6,000 m), vampire squid, lanternfish, giant isopod… each must get a sourced depth before it goes in.
